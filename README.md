# HappyPenHolder
It's a pen holder shaped like Happy with a coaster attachment on the bottom. There are currently two iterations of the holder, one with an round snout and one with just a nose and mouth. Both are cute and somewhat pleasant. Overall, took lots of fillets and extrudes but officially complete. There aren't as many smooth spherical shapes as wanted but attempts were made. Just a note that the print may lead to error due to somewhat not typical shapes(aka a cube).
